Trump's Wacky Challenge

A fast, silly tapping game for iPhone. Pick a ridiculous prop — a giant foam glove, a fluffy pillow or a cream pie — and tap as fast as you can to drain his Composure Meter before the 30-second timer runs out. Win and he takes a silly tumble, feet up and seeing stars.

An unofficial cartoon comedy game celebrating larger-than-life political personalities through ridiculous challenges and slapstick fun. Made for laughs, with no affiliation or endorsement implied.
