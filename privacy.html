TRUMP'S WACKY CHALLENGE

Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 28, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Trump's Wacky Challenge ("the app", "we"), made by ChowChow Labs, handles information. We do not collect your personal information ourselves; the ads shown in the free version are served by Google AdMob (see below).

Information we collect

The app has no accounts and does not ask for your name, email address, contacts, photos or any other personal details.

The free version shows advertising provided by Google AdMob. Premium users see no ads. To show ads, Google and its partners may collect and use your device's advertising identifier (IDFA, only if you allow tracking when asked), other device identifiers, IP address-based approximate location, information about how you interact with ads, and diagnostic information, as described in Google's advertising policies and Privacy Policy. We do not receive this information and do not use analytics tools.

Tracking choices

On first launch you may be asked whether to allow tracking across other companies' apps and websites, and for consent where required by law. You can decline and still play with non-personalized ads, and you can change this at any time in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.

Data stored on your device

Your settings, chosen prop, level progress and any Premium day pass are stored only on your device. They are not sent to us. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Sharing results

When you choose to share a result, the app creates an image on your device and opens Apple's share sheet. Nothing is posted unless you pick a destination; what happens next is governed by the app or service you choose.

Our apps list

The About screen downloads a public list of ChowChow Labs apps and websites from chowchowlabs.ca. This request contains no personal information.

Purchases

Purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive or store your payment card or Apple Account details. Apple's handling of your information is described in Apple's Privacy Policy.

Contacting support

If you email us, we receive your email address and the contents of your message, and we use them only to respond to you.

Children's privacy

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The effective date above shows when it was last changed.
