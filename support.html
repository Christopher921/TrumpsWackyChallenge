How do I play?

Tap Start, pick a prop, pick a scene, then tap the big TAP FAST button as quickly as you can. Each separate press counts once — holding the button does nothing. His composure recovers when you slow down, so keep a fast pace. Beat a level to unlock the next (Gentle, Standard, Hard).

What does Premium include?

Premium is a one-time purchase that unlocks the Saucepan, Rubber Chicken and Baseball Bat and removes all ads. You can also watch three short videos in the app to get Premium free for 24 hours.

How do I restore Premium?

Open the app, tap the gear icon on the Home screen and choose Restore purchase (it's also on the Go Premium screen). Make sure you are signed in with the Apple Account used for the original purchase.

Why does the free version show ads?

The free version shows a banner ad on the Home screen and one full-screen ad after each finished game, provided by Google AdMob. On first launch you may be asked whether to allow tracking, and for consent where required by law; you can decline and still play with non-personalized ads. Premium removes all ads. You can change your tracking choice in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.

I can't hear any sound

Check that Sound is on in the app's Settings and that your volume is up.
